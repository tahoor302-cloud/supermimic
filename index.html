    06 — About

    Your partnerin China

    SikandarTech is run by a sourcing professional based in Guangzhou, China. For more than 20 years we have helped importers, wholesalers and online sellers in the USA, UK, Europe, UAE, Asia, Africa and around the world buy the latest technology and gadgets directly from manufacturers.

    We visit the markets and factories, negotiate prices, inspect quality before shipment and handle logistics end to end, so you get genuine products at factory prices without the risk.

    • 01Product sourcing

      Reliable manufacturers for any gadget, with compared quotes.

    • 02Quality inspection

      Pre-production, in-production and pre-shipment checks with photo and video reports.

    • 03Factory verification

      On-site audits to confirm a real factory, not a middleman.

    • 04Shipping & logistics

      Sea, air and express, consolidation and door-to-door delivery.

    • 05OEM / private label

      Your logo and packaging, managed with the factory.

    • 06China market tours

      Guided visits to Guangzhou and Shenzhen electronics markets and fairs.
